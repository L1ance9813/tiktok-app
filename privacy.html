Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

The Grumpy Old Grandpa Content Pipeline ("the Service") is a local application that runs on your own computer. It does not have remote servers or databases that collect or store your personal information.

2. Data Processed Locally

The Service processes video files, images, and metadata that you provide. All processing occurs locally on your machine. Temporary copies of your files may be created during processing and are deleted after publishing. Files in the uploads/ and processed/ directories are cleaned up automatically.

3. Third-Party API Access

The Service communicates with YouTube and TikTok APIs only when you initiate an upload. When you upload content:

• Your video and metadata are sent directly to the platform you selected (YouTube and/or TikTok) via their official APIs.

• OAuth tokens are stored locally in the config/ directory. These tokens grant the Service permission to upload on your behalf. You can revoke these tokens at any time through your Google or TikTok account settings.

4. No Data Collection

The Service does not:

• Collect analytics or usage data
• Send telemetry or crash reports
• Track your activity
• Share your data with third parties (beyond the platforms you explicitly select)
• Store your content on any remote server

5. Data Retention

Upload history is stored locally in config/history.json for your convenience (limited to 50 entries). OAuth tokens are stored locally and can be deleted at any time. No data is stored on remote servers.

6. Security

Since the Service runs locally, data security depends on your system's security. OAuth tokens are stored as plain text in your local config directory. We recommend ensuring your system is secure and not sharing access to the config directory.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
